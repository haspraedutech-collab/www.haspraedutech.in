Who we are

Who is HasPra EduTech?

HasPra EduTech connects learners, educators, professionals and institutions through technical training, seminars, workshops, conferences and faculty development programmes.

We aim to serve school and college students, research scholars, faculty members, teachers, professionals and institutions through technical training, seminars, workshops, conferences, faculty development programmes and research article writing support.

We create opportunities to share expertise, explore emerging technologies and strengthen collaboration between institutions and industry.

Research & academic support

Helping ideas develop into stronger research

Structured support for learning the research process, using analytical tools and communicating work clearly and ethically.

📚

Research Methodology

Research questions, literature mapping, objectives, methodology planning and experimental design fundamentals.

📊

Data & Modelling Tools

Learning-oriented sessions on data processing, statistics, visualisation and computational modelling tools.

All support is intended to strengthen independent learning and responsible scholarship. Authorship, results, citations and research data must remain genuine; publication or acceptance is never guaranteed.

MoU & Collaborations

Building partnerships with colleges, universities, research institutions and industry to support technical training, knowledge exchange, joint programmes and innovation.

HasPra EduTech welcomes collaboration with colleges, universities, research institutions, schools, industries and other organisations to promote academic excellence, technical innovation and professional development.

We aim to establish Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) for joint workshops, seminars, conferences, faculty development programmes, student training, research activities, industry-oriented projects and knowledge-sharing initiatives.

Let us work together to build meaningful partnerships that create opportunities for students, faculty members, researchers and industry professionals.
